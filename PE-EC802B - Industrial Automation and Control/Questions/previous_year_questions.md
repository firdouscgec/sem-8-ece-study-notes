### Group-A (Very Short Answer Type Questions)
**1. Answer any ten of the following:** `[1 x 10 = 10]`
*   **(I)** Cohen-Coon tuning rules are used for \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ systems.
* **Answer:**  **Process with time-lag and self-regulation.**
*   **(II)** Which control valve is used for pressure control?
* **Answer:**  **Globe valve**
*   **(III)** What type of controller is used for the elimination of offset?
* **Answer:**  **Integral controller (I-controller)**
*   **(IV)** The operating frequency of an ultrasonic sensor is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
* **Answer:**  **10–65 kHz**
*   **(V)** A cascade controller is used when the process shows \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
* **Answer:**  **Slow response and large time lag**
*   **(VI)** The primary sensing element of the pressure thermometer is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
* **Answer:**  **Pressure bulb filled with a liquid**
*   **(VII)** The Ziegler-Nichols tuning method is used for \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
* **Answer:**  **Process with time-lag and self-regulation**
*   **(VIII)** The derivative mode (D controller) is also known as \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
* **Answer:**  **Rate controller**
*   **(IX)** If the proportional band of an electronic PID controller is set at 10, then what is the proportional gain?
* **Answer:**  **10**
*   **(X)** Cohen-Coon tuning rules are used for \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ systems. *(Note: This was repeated in the original question paper)*
* **Answer:**  **Process with time-lag and self-regulation**
*   **(XI)** Thermocouples generate output voltage according to the \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
* **Answer:**  **Seebeck effect**
*   **(XII)** Touch screen devices use which sensor?
* **Answer:**  **Capacitive sensors**

### Group-A (Very Short Answer Type Questions)
**1. Answer any ten of the following:** `[1 x 10 = 10]`
*   **(I)** The quarter-amplitude decay ratio is basically a design criterion specified by the Ziegler-Nichols method which implies that the amplitude of an oscillation must be reduced by a factor of \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
* **Answer:**  **1/4**
*   **(II)** Database management and decisions regarding production are handled in which level of the DCS structure?
* **Answer:**  **Production control level**
*   **(III)** A summing amplifier block followed by an inverter block represents a \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ controller.
* **Answer:**  **PI controller**
*   **(IV)** What are the advantages of a pneumatic controller?
* **Answer:**  **Simple in construction, robust, no need for electrical supply**
*   **(V)** Which frequency is attenuated in a Low-Pass filter?
* **Answer:**  **Higher frequencies**
*   **(VI)** Which of the following controllers has the maximum offset?
    *   (a) P-controller
    *   (b) P-I controller
    *   (c) P-I-D controller
    *   (d) P-D controller
* **Answer:**  **(a) P-controller**
*   **(VII)** What are the components of a SCADA system?
* **Answer:**  **RTU, communication network, host system**
*   **(VIII)** What is the purpose of a pressure gauge in a boiler plant?
* **Answer:**  **To measure pressure and display it**
*   **(IX)** State the cause for using starters with D.C. motors.
* **Answer:**  **To limit high starting current**
*   **(X)** When a control valve plug is fully connected to the \_\_\_\_\_\_\_, then it is fully closed.
* **Answer:**  **Valve seat**
*   **(XI)** In an electronic Integral controller, if the resistance and capacitance values are both doubled, then the controller response time will be \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ and the controller gain will be \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
* **Answer:**  **Doubled, halved**
*   **(XII)** State the load disturbances in the feed-forward control strategy of a simple heat exchanger.
* **Answer:**  **Inlet temperature, flow rate, and composition of feed fluid**

### Group-A (Very Short Answer Type Questions)
**1. Answer any ten of the following:** `[1 x 10 = 10]`
*   **(I)** Which control scheme is known as anticipatory control?
* **Answer:**  **Feed-forward control**
*   **(II)** If the Proportional Band (PB) of a P-controller is very wide, then the system response is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
* **Answer:**  **Slow**
*   **(III)** The function of reset action in a process controller is to \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
* **Answer:**  **Eliminate offset**
*   **(IV)** When should Cascade Control be used?
* **Answer:**  **When the process has slow response and large time lag**
*   **(V)** Which controllers do not show a continuous action?
* **Answer:**  **Digital controllers**
*   **(VI)** The response of Feed-forward control is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ than Feedback control.
* **Answer:**  **Faster**
*   **(VII)** What is the transfer function of a dead-time element?
* **Answer:**  **e^(-sTd)**
*   **(VIII)** Provide an example of a self-regulating system.
* **Answer:**  **Liquid level in a tank with constant outflow**
*   **(IX)** A ratio control system is a special type of \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ control system.
* **Answer:**  **Feed-forward control system**
*   **(X)** The span of the pneumatic signal used in industry is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
* **Answer:**  **3-15 psi**
*   **(XI)** The purpose of ratio control is to \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
* **Answer:**  **Maintain a constant ratio between two process variables**
*   **(XII)** The gauge factor of a strain gauge indicates its \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
* **Answer:**  **Sensitivity**
