# OE-EC804A: Artificial Intelligence

*   **L-T-P:** 3L:0T:0P
*   **Credits:** 3

## Syllabus

**Unit I: Introduction**
Overview; Foundation; History; The State of Art.

**Unit II: Intelligent Agents**
Agents and environment; Rationality; The nature of environment; The structure of agents.

**Unit III: Solving Problems by Searching**
Problem-solving agents; Well-defined problems & solutions; Formulating problems; Searching for solution; Uninformed search strategies: (BFS, DFS, DLS, IDDFS, Bidirectional Search).

**Unit IV: Informed Search & Exploration**
Informed search strategies; Heuristic functions; On-line search agents and unknown environment.

**Unit V: Constraint Satisfaction Problems**
Constraint satisfaction problems; Backtracking search for CSPs; Local search for CSPs.

**Unit VI: Adversarial Search**
Games; Optimal decisions in games; Alpha-Beta pruning.

**Unit VII: Logical Agents**
Knowledge-based agents; The wumpus world as an example world; Logic: Propositional logic, Reasoning patterns in propositional logic.

**Unit VIII: First-Order Logic**
Syntax and semantics of first-order logic; Use of first-order logic.

## Course Outcomes
At the end of the course, the students will be able to:
1.  Understand the modern view of AI as the study of agents that receive percepts from the environment and perform actions.
2.  Demonstrate awareness of the major challenges facing AI and the complexity of typical problems within the field.
3.  Exhibit strong familiarity with a number of important AI techniques, including in particular search, knowledge representation, planning and constraint management.
4.  Assess critically the techniques presented and apply them to real-world problems.
