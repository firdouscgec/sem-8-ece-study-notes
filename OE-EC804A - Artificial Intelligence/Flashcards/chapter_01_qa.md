# Flashcards: Unit I - Introduction to AI

These active recall Question-and-Answer cards are designed to test your memory on the core definitions, structures, and systems of Unit I.

---

### 🎴 Card 1: Definition of AI
*   **Question:** What is the formal definition of Artificial Intelligence?
*   **Answer:** A branch of computer science concerned with building autonomous systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as visual perception, decision-making, learning, reasoning, and natural language understanding.

---

### 🎴 Card 2: The Four Approaches to AI
*   **Question:** What are the four conceptual approaches to defining AI?
*   **Answer:**
    1.  **Thinking Humanly:** Modeling cognitive thought processes.
    2.  **Thinking Rationally:** Formulating logic/laws of thought.
    3.  **Acting Humanly:** Behaving indistinguishably from humans (Turing Test).
    4.  **Acting Rationally:** Operating as a rational agent to maximize utility.

---

### 🎴 Card 3: Turing Test Setup & Criteria
*   **Question:** What is the setup and success criterion of the Turing Test?
*   **Answer:**
    *   **Setup:** A human interrogator communicates textually with one human and one computer in separate rooms.
    *   **Criterion:** If the interrogator cannot reliably tell the computer apart from the human after 5 minutes of questioning, the computer passes.

---

### 🎴 Card 4: Six Capabilities for Turing Test
*   **Question:** What are the six key capabilities a machine needs to pass the total Turing Test?
*   **Answer:**
    1.  **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**
    2.  **Knowledge Representation**
    3.  **Automated Reasoning**
    4.  **Machine Learning**
    5.  **Computer Vision**
    6.  **Robotics**

---

### 🎴 Card 5: Core Components of an Expert System
*   **Question:** What are the three primary components of an Expert System?
*   **Answer:**
    1.  **Knowledge Base:** Warehouse of domain facts, heuristics, and IF-THEN rules.
    2.  **Inference Engine:** The reasoning mechanism that applies rules to deduce answers (via forward or backward chaining).
    3.  **User Interface:** The communication portal that allows non-experts to query the system and view its explanations.

---

### 🎴 Card 6: Forward vs. Backward Chaining
*   **Question:** Differentiate between Forward Chaining and Backward Chaining in an Inference Engine.
*   **Answer:**
    *   **Forward Chaining (Data-Driven):** Starts with known facts and applies rules iteratively to infer new facts until a goal or conclusion is reached.
    *   **Backward Chaining (Goal-Driven):** Starts with a goal or hypothesis and works backward to find supporting facts/premises in the database.

---

### 🎴 Card 7: Expert System Advantages
*   **Question:** List 5 major advantages of using an Expert System.
*   **Answer:**
    1.  **High Availability:** Accessible 24/7.
    2.  **Consistency:** Eliminates fatigue, stress, and bias.
    3.  **Speed:** Processes complex rules in milliseconds.
    4.  **Preserves Expertise:** Retains organizational knowledge permanently.
    5.  **Explanatory Power:** Explains "why" or "how" it reached a conclusion.

---

### 🎴 Card 8: Knowledge Acquisition Bottleneck
*   **Question:** What is the "knowledge acquisition bottleneck" in Expert Systems?
*   **Answer:** The extremely slow, tedious, and difficult process of extracting implicit knowledge and heuristics from human experts and translating them into formal, explicit IF-THEN rules for the knowledge base.
