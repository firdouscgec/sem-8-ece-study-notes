## 📅 Unit I: Introduction

### 📄 Short Answers (5 Marks)
*   **[5M][★★★★★] Q1.1:** Draw the block diagrams of a general communication system and a fiber-optic communication system. Explain each block in detail and list the major advantages of using optical fiber cables over copper cables.
*   **[5M][★★★★★] Q1.2:** State the law of refraction (Snell's Law). Draw and explain the concepts of critical angle, acceptance angle, and Numerical Aperture (NA) for an optical fiber.
*   **[5M][★★★★] Q1.3:** Differentiate between meridional rays and skew rays of light propagating inside the core of a circular optical waveguide.
*   **[5M][★★★★] Q1.4:** Explain the propagation of light in a cylindrical dielectric rod using both the ray model and the wave model, addressing the vector nature of light.

### 📄 Long Answers (10 to 15 Marks)
*   **[10M][★★★★★] Q1.5:** Derive a rigorous mathematical expression for the Acceptance Angle and Numerical Aperture (NA) of a multimode step-index fiber in terms of core and cladding refractive indices ($n_1, n_2$).
*   **[10M][★★★★] Q1.6:** Numerical Problems:
    *   **(a)** Calculate the NA of a silica fiber with $n_1 = 1.48$ and $n_2 = 1.46$. What should be the new value of $n_2$ to increase the NA to $0.23$?
    *   **(b)** A typical fiber has $n_1 = 1.82$ and $n_2 = 1.73$. What is the critical angle at the core-cladding interface?
    *   **(c)** A silica optical fiber with core $n_1 = 1.50$ and cladding $n_2 = 1.47$ is analyzed using ray theory. Determine the critical angle, the NA, and the acceptance angle in air.

## 📅 Unit III: Signal Degradation

### 📄 Short Answers (5 Marks)

*   **[5M][★★★★★] Q3.2:** Discuss the attenuation characteristics of a Single-Mode Fiber (SMF) as a function of wavelength. Draw the attenuation curve and detail the three standard transmission windows used in optical communication.
*   **[5M][★★★★] Q3.3:** Discuss the fabrication processes of optical fibers (including vapor deposition techniques and the double-crucible melting method for multicomponent glass).

### 📄 Long Answers (10 to 15 Marks)
*   **[15M][★★★★★] Q3.5:** Dispersion & Pulse Broadening:
    *   **(a)** Explain what is meant by pulse broadening. Detail its effect on the information carrying capacity and bandwidth-distance product of a fiber.
    *   **(b)** Distinguish between intermodal dispersion and intramodal dispersion (material dispersion and waveguide dispersion). What are dispersion-flattened and dispersion-shifted fibers?
    *   **(c)** Derive expressions for the delay difference between the slowest and fastest modes at the fiber output and the rms pulse broadening due to intermodal dispersion on a multimode step-index fiber.
    *   **(d)** A $6\text{ km}$ optical link consists of a multimode step-index fiber with a core refractive index of $1.5$ and a relative index difference of $1\%$. Estimate the intermodal delay difference and the rms pulse broadening.
*   **[10M][★★★★★] Q3.6:** Working Principle of OTDR:
    *   **(a)** Explain the working principle of an Optical Time Domain Reflectometer (OTDR) with the help of a block diagram.
    *   **(b)** How does an OTDR detect fiber faults, splices, bends, and measure attenuation along the link? Sketch a typical OTDR trace.
*   **[5M][★★★★] Q3.7:** Fiber Measurement Techniques:
    *   **(a)** Describe the **Cut-Back Method** for measuring attenuation in an optical fiber.
    *   **(b)** Explain how the **Numerical Aperture (NA)** of a fiber can be measured using its far-field radiation pattern.
    *   **(c)** Briefly discuss the measurement of **chromatic dispersion** in the time or frequency domain.
