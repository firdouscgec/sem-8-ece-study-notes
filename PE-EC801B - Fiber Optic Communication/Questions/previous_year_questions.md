### Group-A (Very Short Answer Type Questions)
**1. Answer any ten of the following:** `[1 x 10 = 10]`
*   **(I)** The number of photons emitted per second for a $2.5\text{ mW}$ He-Ne laser is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
    *   **Answer:** $4.5\times 10^{16}$ photons/sec
*   **(II)** What is the name of the device that distributes light from a main fiber into one or more branch fibers?
    *   **Answer:** Optical Fiber Splitter
*   **(III)** For single-mode step index fibers, the V-number should be less than \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
    *   **Answer:** 2.405
*   **(IV)** The material used for the fabrication of the inner core of an optical fiber is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
    *   **Answer:** Quartz/Silicon Dioxide ($SiO_2$)
*   **(V)** Light is propagated within the fiber core by the phenomenon of \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
    *   **Answer:** Total Internal Reflection (TIR)
*   **(VI)** In an optical fiber, the outer layer is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ and the inner layer is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
    *   **Answer:** Cladding, Core
*   **(VII) ** The maximum wavelength of photons that can be detected by a photodiode made by a semiconductor of bandgap $2\text{ eV}$ is about \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
    *   **Answer:** 620 nm
*   **(VIII)** Which type of fiber-optic coupler causes the distribution of optical power from more than two input ports among several output ports?
    *   **Answer:** $N \times M$ Multimode Coupler
*   **(IX)** Which detector gives an amplified output?
    *   **Answer:**  Avalanche Photodiode (APD)
*   **(X)** Unlike wired media, optical fibers are highly resistant to \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
    *   **Answer:** Electromagnetic Interference (EMI)
*   **(XI)** Which mechanism is used in Laser Technology for the generation of light?
    *   **Answer:** Stimulated Emission
*   **(XII)** A typical optical fiber cable has specified values of $n_1 = 1.82$ and $n_2 = 1.73$. What will be the critical angle of incidence?
    *   **Answer:** $\theta_c = \cos^{-1}\left(\frac{n_2}{n_1}\right) = \cos^{-1}\left(\frac{1.73}{1.82}\right) \approx 18.47^\circ$

### Group-A (Very Short Answer Type Questions)
**1. Answer any ten of the following:** `[1 x 10 = 10]`
*   **(I)** What is meant by Bit Error Rate (BER)?
    *   **Answer:** The probability of a bit error (a 1 being received as a 0, or vice versa) in a digital communication system.
*   **(II)** What are the characteristics of a photodiode?
    *   **Answer:** High quantum efficiency, fast response time, low noise, and good spectral response.
*   **(III)** What are optical couplers?
    *   **Answer:** Devices that divide or combine optical signals, such as splitters, combiners, and WDM couplers.
*   **(IV)** What is the difference between spontaneous emission and stimulated emission?
    *   **Answer:** Spontaneous emission is random light emission from excited atoms, while stimulated emission is light emission triggered by an incident photon (the basis of lasers).
*   **(V)** What is an optical communication system?
    *   **Answer:** A communication system that uses optical fibers to transmit information via light signals.
*   **(VI)** What is the working principle of a Light Emitting Diode (LED)?
    *   **Answer:** Injection electroluminescence in a forward-biased semiconductor p-n junction.
*   **(VII)** Which is the transmission medium between the transmitter and receiver in fiber optics?
    *   **Answer:** Optical Fiber Cable (Glass or Plastic)
*   **(VIII)** Which type of injection laser involves the use of geometry for fabrication of the multimode injection laser with a single or small number of lateral modes?
    *   **Answer:** Stripe Geometry Injection Laser
*   **(IX)** How many mating cycles are typically rated by matched SC Connectors?
    *   **Answer:** \~1000 mating cycles
*   **(X)** Which among the following is regarded as a keyed bayonet connector along with its feasibility of easiest insertion and removal from the fiber optic cable?
    *   **Answer:** Bayonet Connector (e.g., Biconic, FC/PC variations)
*   **(XI)** Multimode step index fiber has a large core diameter typically in the range of \_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
    *   **Answer:** 50 to 100 \(\mu\text{m}\)
*   **(XII)** Find the performance characteristics of multimode graded index fibers.
    *   **Answer:** Reduced intermodal dispersion (compared to step-index), larger bandwidth, and easier coupling.

    *   **(a)** Discuss, with the aid of a suitable diagram, one melting method for the preparation of multicomponent glass. `[5]`
    *   **(b)** Briefly describe the major reasons for the cabling of optical fibers which are to be placed in a field environment. `[5]`
    *   **(c)** Explain the working principle of an Optical Time Domain Reflectometer (OTDR). `[5]`
*   **11.**
    *   **(a)** Mention the criteria for choosing the photo detectors for optical communication. `[5]`
    *   **(b)** Define Responsivity and Quantum Efficiency of a photo detector. Derive an expression for the Responsivity of an intrinsic photodiode in terms of Quantum Efficiency and the wavelength of the incident radiation. `[7]`
    *   **(c)** When $3 \times 10^{11}$ photons (each with wavelength $0.85\ \mu\text{m}$) are incident on a photodiode, on average $1.2 \times 10^{11}$ electrons are collected at the terminals of the device. Determine the quantum efficiency and responsivity of the photodiode at $0.85\ \mu\text{m}$. `[3]`
    
    *   **Answer:**
        *   **(a)**
            *   **Answer:** Criteria for Choosing the Photo detectors for Optical Communication:
            1.  **Spectral Response**: The photodetector must have a spectral response that matches the wavelength of the incoming light.
            2.  **Quantum Efficiency**: The photodetector should have a high quantum efficiency, ideally close to 1.
            3.  **Responsivity**: The photodetector should have a high responsivity, ideally close to 1.
            4.  **Dark Current**: The photodetector should have a low dark current.
            5.  **Response Time**: The photodetector should have a fast response time.
            6.  **Bandwidth**: The photodetector should have a wide bandwidth.
            7.  **Sensitivity**: The photodetector should have a high sensitivity.
            8.  **Dynamic Range**: The photodetector should have a wide dynamic range.
            9.  **Noise**: The photodetector should have low noise.
            10. **Cost**: The photodetector should be cost-effective.
        *   **(b)**
            *   **Answer:**
                *   **Quantum Efficiency, $\eta$:**
                    $\eta = \frac{\text{Number of electrons collected}}{\text{Number of incident photons}} = \frac{n_e}{n_{ph}}$
                *   **Responsivity, R:**
                    $R = \frac{\text{Photocurrent (I)}}{\text{Optical power incident (P)}} = \frac{I}{P}$
            
            Derivation of R in terms of $\eta$ and $\lambda$:
            The power of the incident light is given by:
            $P = \frac{n_{ph} \times h \times c}{\lambda}$
            
            The photocurrent generated is given by:
            $I = \frac{n_e \times e}{t}$
            
            Substituting the values of P and I into the responsivity equation:
            $R = \frac{\frac{n_e \times e}{t}}{\frac{n_{ph} \times h \times c}{\lambda}} = \frac{n_e \times e \times \lambda}{n_{ph} \times h \times c}$
            
            Using the quantum efficiency equation, we can write:
            $n_e = \eta \times n_{ph}$
            
            Substituting this into the responsivity equation:
            $R = \frac{\eta \times n_{ph} \times e \times \lambda}{n_{ph} \times h \times c} = \frac{\eta \times e \times \lambda}{h \times c}$
            
            Where:
            *   $n_e$ = Number of electrons collected
            *   $n_{ph}$ = Number of incident photons
            *   $e$ = Charge of an electron = $1.602 \times 10^{-19}$ C
            *   $\lambda$ = Wavelength of the incident light in meters
            *   $h$ = Planck's constant = $6.626 \times 10^{-34}$ J·s
            *   $c$ = Speed of light = $3 \times 10^8$ m/s
            
            *   **(c)**
            *   **Answer:**
                *   Quantum Efficiency, $\eta = \frac{1.2 \times 10^{11}}{3 \times 10^{11}} = 0.4$
                *   Responsivity, $R = \frac{\eta \times e \times \lambda}{h \times c} = \frac{0.4 \times 1.602 \times 10^{-19} \times 0.85 \times 10^{-6}}{6.626 \times 10^{-34} \times 3 \times 10^8} = 0.431$ A/W
