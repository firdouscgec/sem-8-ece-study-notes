### 📄 Very Short Answers (1 Mark)

*   **[1M][★★★]** A typical optical fiber cable has specified values of $n_1 = 1.82$ and $n_2 = 1.73$. What will be the critical angle of incidence? (Ans: **$\theta_c = \sin^{-1}\left(\frac{n_2}{n_1}\right) = \sin^{-1}\left(\frac{1.73}{1.82}\right) \approx 71.9^\circ$**) [2024–25]
*   **[1M][★★★]** What is an optical communication system? (Ans: **A communication system that uses optical fibers to transmit information via light signals.**) [2023–24]
*   **[1M][★★★]** Which is the transmission medium between the transmitter and receiver in fiber optics? (Ans: **Optical Fiber Cable (Glass or Plastic)**) [2023–24]
*   **[1M][★★★★]** Define the Acceptance Angle of an optical fiber. (Ans: **The maximum angle of incidence at the core-cladding interface for which light is totally internally reflected and thus propagates through the fiber.**) [2022–23]
